# I Will Get It

> *"No matter if the earth is on fire, I will spend every last breath of my life only protecting and being there for her."*

He finally asked her out. She said yes. The night before the date, he and his best friend climb the hill
to watch a shooting-star cluster that comes once every thousand years — and he won't stop promising the
impossible: slay the dragon, fight the aliens, climb the mountains, run through the forest.

The comet heard every word.

**I Will Get It** is a genre-hopping 3D adventure where every level is one of his promises, each playing
like a different kind of game, with his best friend guiding him by voice from the hill.

## Status

Early development.

## Tech

- Engine: [Godot 4.7.1](https://godotengine.org)
- Target: Windows, macOS, Linux

## Running

1. Install Godot 4.7.1.
2. Open Godot → **Import** → select this folder's `project.godot`.
3. Press **F5**.
